About Us

Sharp Shark Infosys

Where Vision Meets Results

Sharp Shark Infosys is a results-driven business solutions company and a USA-based outsourcing and offshoring company delivering business process outsourcing (BPO), technology solutions, and operational support from Dhaka, Bangladesh, to businesses in the USA and international markets.

We combine business process expertise, technology, creative solutions, and operational support to help organizations streamline their operations, expand their reach, reduce operational costs, and achieve sustainable growth.

We specialize in International Telemarketing, B2B Lead Generation, Property Preservation, Property Maintenance, Software Development, AI & Automation, Web Development, Website Design, Video Editing, and Graphic Design. Our goal is to empower businesses with practical solutions, effective communication, modern technology, and strategic execution that turn opportunities into measurable results.
